Welcome to **Earth and Faith**.

This is my personal blog. I write about religion, science, philosophy and the big questions that people around the world have asked for thousands of years: Where do we come from? What is true? How should we live?

## What you will find here

- Clear explanations of religious ideas, texts and history
- Discussions about science, evidence and critical thinking
- Honest questions, doubts and personal reflections
- Thoughts on living a good life without relying on blind belief

## My approach

I write from a secular and skeptical point of view. I believe every idea should be open to questions and evidence. But I also believe in treating people with respect. On this blog I criticize ideas, not people.

Whenever I make a claim, I try to explain my reasoning and point to reliable sources. If I get something wrong, please tell me and I will correct it.

## Who this blog is for

This blog is for readers everywhere: believers, non-believers and the curious. You do not have to agree with me. I only ask that you read with an open mind.

If you would like to share your thoughts, visit the [Contact](/contact/) page.

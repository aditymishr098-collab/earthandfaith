    How to read this map

    • Each dot marks the core region of a civilization, not its full borders. Empires grew and shrank over time.
    • Dates are approximate and scholars sometimes disagree. Many dates come from archaeology, not from written records.
    • This is a selection of famous examples. Many other societies, such as the many peoples of Africa, Australia and the Americas, are not shown. Their absence is a limit of this list, not a statement about them.
    • BCE means "before the common era", the years counted backwards from year 1.

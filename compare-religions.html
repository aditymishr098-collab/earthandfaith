Choose two religions and see them side by side. Each answer is a short, simplified summary, and the religions themselves are more varied than any table can show.

Follower numbers are rounded estimates, mostly based on Pew Research Center figures for 2020. Dates for the oldest traditions are debated by scholars. If you spot a mistake, please write to me through the Contact page.

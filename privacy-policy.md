*Last updated: October 3, 2026*

Your privacy matters to me. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit **earthandfaith.online** ("this site").

## Information I collect

This site does not use analytics, tracking tools, advertising, user accounts, comments or forms. I do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information through the site itself.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). I do not receive or see this information.

## Language translation

This site offers a language menu that uses **Google Translate**. When the menu loads, scripts are requested from Google, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. If you choose a language, Google may store a cookie in your browser to remember your choice. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details. If you prefer not to use this service, do not open the language menu. Translations are automatic and may contain errors; the English text is the original.

## Other services

The World Religion Map, World Hardship Map and Civilizations pages load a software library from **cdnjs** and map outlines from **jsDelivr**. The World Hardship Map also requests statistics from the **World Bank** (api.worldbank.org). These services may receive your IP address and browser details when a map loads.

## Offline storage

This site uses a small service worker that saves copies of pages in your browser so the site loads faster and works offline. This stays on your device, is not sent to me, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

## Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. The only cookie that may be used is the one Google Translate can set if you choose a language.

## Emails you send me

If you email me at contact@earthandfaith.online, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use this only to read and reply to your message. I do not sell, rent or share it with anyone, and I may delete old messages from time to time.

## External links

Some posts link to other websites. I am not responsible for the content or the privacy practices of those websites.

## Children

This site is a general-interest blog and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices

You can use this site without sending me any information. If you have emailed me and would like your messages deleted, write to me and I will remove them.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the [Contact](/contact/) page.

*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

Your privacy matters to me. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit **earthandfaith.online** ("this site").

## Information I collect

This site does not have accounts, comments, sign-up forms, advertising or analytics tools of its own. I do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information through the site itself.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Language translation and cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. It offers a language menu that uses **Google Translate**. When the menu loads, scripts are requested from Google, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. If you choose a language, Google may store a cookie in your browser to remember your choice. Please see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details. If you prefer not to use this service, simply do not open the language menu, or block scripts from Google in your browser.

## Emails you send me

If you email me at contact@earthandfaith.online, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use this only to read and reply to your message. I do not sell, rent or share it with anyone, and I may delete old messages from time to time.

## External links

Some posts link to other websites, such as books, articles or research. I am not responsible for the content or the privacy practices of those websites.

## Children

This site is a general-interest blog and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices

Because I do not collect personal information through the site, there is nothing for me to delete or export about you from here. If you have emailed me and would like your messages deleted, write to me and I will remove them.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time, for example if I add new features to the site. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the [Contact](/contact/) page.

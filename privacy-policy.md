*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

Your privacy matters to me. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit **earthandfaith.online** ("this site").

## Information I collect

This site has no user accounts, comments or advertising. Information is collected only in the ways described below: visitor statistics (with your consent) and the question form.

## Visitor statistics (Google Analytics)

This site uses **Google Analytics** to count visits and to understand which pages are read. Analytics is loaded **only if you click "Accept"** on the notice that appears at the bottom of the page. If you click "Decline", Google Analytics is not loaded. When it is active, Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, your device and browser type, and your approximate location. I use these statistics only to understand which posts are useful. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

I also use **Google Search Console**, which shows me how this site appears in Google Search, such as which searches lead to my pages. It does not add anything to your browser.

## Question form (Google Forms)

If you send a question through the "Ask a Question" page, the form is provided by **Google Forms**. Your question, and your name and email if you choose to give them, are stored by Google and made available to me. I use this information only to read your question and, if you gave an email address, to reply. I will not use your name or email in a post without your permission. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Language translation

This site offers a language menu that uses **Google Translate**. When the menu loads, scripts are requested from Google, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. If you choose a language, Google may store a cookie in your browser to remember your choice. Translations are automatic and may contain errors; the English text is the original.

## Other services

The World Religion Map page loads a software library from **cdnjs** and map outlines from **jsDelivr**. These services may receive your IP address and browser details when the map loads.

## Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. Cookies may be set by Google if you accept analytics, choose a language in the translation menu, or use the question form. This site stores your analytics choice (accept or decline) in your browser's local storage so that it does not ask you every time.

## Emails you send me

If you email me at contact@earthandfaith.online, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use this only to read and reply to your message. I do not sell, rent or share it with anyone, and I may delete old messages from time to time.

## External links

Some posts link to other websites. I am not responsible for the content or the privacy practices of those websites.

## Children

This site is a general-interest blog and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices

You can use this site without sending me any information and without accepting analytics. If you have sent me a question or an email and would like it deleted, write to me and I will remove it.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time, for example if I add new features to the site. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the [Contact](/contact/) page.

Welcome, and thank you for visiting.

**Earth and Faith** is a place where I think out loud about some of the oldest questions in human history. What do we know about the world, and how do we know it? Why do so many people around the globe believe in a higher power? What role does religion play in our lives, and what happens when we look at it with evidence and reason?

## Why I started this blog

I believe that questions are never dangerous. Ideas that are true can survive being examined, and ideas that cannot survive examination deserve a closer look. I also believe that knowledge should be shared freely, in simple words, with anyone who is curious.

## What to expect

In the coming weeks and months I will write about:

- The history of major religions and their texts
- What science says about the universe, life and the human mind
- Common myths, superstitions and misunderstandings
- Ethics and meaning without religion
- Personal reflections and reader questions

## One promise

I will always try to criticize ideas, not people. Behind every belief there is a human being who deserves respect.

If you have a question or a topic you would like me to explore, please use the [Contact](/contact/) page.

Thank you for reading. This is only the beginning.

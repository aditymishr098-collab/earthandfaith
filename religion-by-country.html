Every article follows the same plan

  1. The people before. Which beliefs were there before the big religions arrived.
  2. How the religions arrived. By trade, conquest, missionaries or local reformers.
  3. What was done in religion's name. Wars, persecutions and massacres, with death-toll estimates and a note on how big religion's role really was.
  4. Claims and evidence. Popular claims about the country's religious past, checked with the same standard for every faith.
  5. Where things stand today. Current numbers from sources such as the Pew Research Center.

Death tolls are estimates and historians often disagree. I say so in each article, and I list the sources. If you want a particular country sooner, or if you find a mistake, please write through the Contact page.

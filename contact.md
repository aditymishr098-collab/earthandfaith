I would love to hear from you. Whether you agree or disagree with something you read here, your message is welcome.

**Email:** [contact@earthandfaith.online](mailto:contact@earthandfaith.online)

## What you can write to me about

- **Questions** about a post or about a topic on this blog (you can also use the [Ask a Question](/ask/) page)
- **Corrections**: if you think I have made a factual mistake, please tell me which post and which sentence, and if possible send a source
- **Ideas for future posts** or topics you would like me to explore
- **Your own story or perspective**, if you would like to share it
- **Feedback** about the design, the translations or anything that does not work well on the site

## Before you write

- I read every message, but I may not be able to reply to all of them, and replies can take a few days.
- Please keep your message respectful. Messages that contain abuse, threats or hate will not receive a reply.
- I do not give personal, legal, medical or religious advice by email.
- If you write to me, I will use your email address only to reply to you. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for details.

## Translations

If you notice a serious error in an automatic translation of this site, please tell me which language and which page. I cannot fix automatic translations, but knowing about a problem helps me decide whether to add a note. The English text is always the original.

Thank you for taking the time to write.

Religions are born, they spread, and some of them fade away. This timeline shows religions that no longer have a continuous community of believers, and a few ancient ones that survive today as very small communities. Pick a filter, then read the notes under each bar.

What these labels mean

  • Faded away: no continuous community of believers remains. The religion is known from texts, ruins and art.
  • Modern revival: the original religion faded, but today some people try to revive it. These revivals are usually small and rebuilt from surviving sources, so they are not identical to the ancient practice.
  • Survives in mixed form: the old religion lost its state and temples, but some beliefs and customs live on, often blended with another religion.
  • Living, very small: an old religion that still has believers today, but only a small community.

Dates are approximate. A religion rarely ends on one day. Usually it fades over generations as rulers, trade, conquest or new faiths change a society. I am describing history, not judging anyone's beliefs, past or present.
